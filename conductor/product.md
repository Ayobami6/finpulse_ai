# Product Definition

## Initial Concept
An AI-powered application for financial analysis and insights.

## Target Users
- Financial Analysts
- Individual Investors
- Businesses/Corporations

## Primary Goals/Value Proposition
The application aims to automate data collection and aggregation from various financial sources, providing a streamlined process for users to gather and consolidate financial information.

## Core Features
The application will provide interactive dashboards for visualizing financial data, enabling users to explore and understand complex financial information through intuitive graphical representations.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Success will be measured by the accuracy of predictive models and insights generated by the application, ensuring that the AI-driven analysis provides reliable and valuable information to users.

## Non-functional Requirements/Constraints
The application must ensure high availability and reliability for continuous access to financial data, guaranteeing that users can consistently access and utilize the platform without interruptions.
